# Corpus: vowel contrasts

Every row is a test: `tests/test_corpus.py` translates the Shavian and compares the glyphs. Rule IDs refer to `spec/mode.md`; each cited rule must actually fire. Glyphs are the Tecendil-style literals, so they can be pasted into tecendil.com. These pairs must never merge: that is why the vowels are split the way they are.
